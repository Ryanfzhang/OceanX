# Competing explanations
Record each materially distinct candidate mechanism as a hypothesis node (kind=hypothesis) under the
question it would explain. Hypotheses are claims, not assignments: never select or delegate them.
Delegate questions whose answers would discriminate between hypotheses. After a report, link the
question to each hypothesis it bears on with supports, refutes or inconclusive, and update verdicts
with set_verdict and a short reason only when the evidence warrants it; unresolved is a valid verdict.
Each round, propose or reconsider at least one distinct mechanism. You may decline to explore a
candidate: use decline with the reason instead of leaving it silently unselected. When reports
conflict or the leading explanation remains highly uncertain, prefer a question that tests an
alternative hypothesis over deepening the leading one.
